ABOUT THE PROJECT

Data insights for
cleaner decisions.

A student data-science project exploring vehicle emissions and prediction.

Project objectives

  • Explore emissions across vehicle classes and fuel types.
  • Study how fuel consumption relates to CO₂ output.
  • Predict CO₂ emissions from vehicle specifications.

Machine learning

Random Forest Regression estimates CO₂ emissions in g/km. Random Forest Classification assigns Low, Medium or High categories.

Low ≤ 220; Medium 221–273; High ≥ 274 g/km.

Dataset & limitations

Dataset: 7,385 vehicle records from the supplied Canada vehicle emissions CSV.

Predictions depend on the data and inputs. Lab/test fuel consumption can differ from real-world driving. Correlation is not causation.
